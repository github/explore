AI governance is the set of policies, frameworks, standards, and practices that guide the responsible development, deployment, and oversight of artificial intelligence systems. It focuses on ensuring AI is ethical, transparent, fair, secure, accountable, and compliant with applicable laws and regulations while minimizing risks and maximizing societal benefits.
