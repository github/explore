Tennis is a racket sport in which players hit a ball over a net into the opponent's court, with matches scored in points, games, and sets. On GitHub, this topic covers software connected with the sport, such as implementations of its scoring system, tools for analyzing match and player statistics, tournament draw and club management applications, and match simulations.
