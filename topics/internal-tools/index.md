Low-Code platforms and Internal Tools encompass modern frameworks and open source solutions designed for the rapid development of admin panels, operational dashboards, and custom business workflows. By minimizing the amount of boilerplate hand-coding required, these development tools empower engineering and operational teams to quickly scaffold mission-critical internal software, allowing them to focus primary development efforts on core, customer-facing product features.
