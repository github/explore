Bootcamps are intensive training programs that provide hands-on experience in a specific field. They are designed to equip participants with the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in a particular industry or profession. Bootcamps often focus on practical learning, offering real-world projects and mentorship opportunities to help learners gain valuable experience.
