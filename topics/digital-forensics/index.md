**Digital forensics** is the process of collecting, preserving, examining, and analyzing digital evidence to investigate security incidents, cybercrime, and other events involving digital systems.

Digital forensics can involve examining computers, mobile devices, networks, cloud environments, storage media, memory, and application artifacts. Practitioners use forensic acquisition, evidence preservation, artifact analysis, and timeline reconstruction to identify relevant activity while maintaining the integrity and provenance of evidence.

It encompasses forensic investigation techniques, analysis tools, evidence management, and methodologies used to reconstruct events and uncover relevant information from digital systems.
