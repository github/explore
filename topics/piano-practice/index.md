Piano practice covers the software that helps people learn and practice the piano: sheet music readers that follow along as you play, apps that check the notes played on a MIDI or acoustic keyboard, metronomes, ear training, sight-reading drills, and scale, arpeggio, and technique exercises. Many of these projects run in the browser with the Web MIDI API, or listen to the instrument through a microphone.
