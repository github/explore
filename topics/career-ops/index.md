career-ops is a free, open source job search system that puts hiring leverage on the candidate's side. It runs inside an AI coding assistant such as Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode, or in an optional local web interface. Paste a job posting and it checks whether the listing is still open, scores the role from 1.0 to 5.0 against your own CV, and recommends against applying below 4.0. It drafts a tailored CV and cover letter and tracks every application on your machine, with no account and no telemetry. It never submits or sends anything. You review every draft and make every decision.

Santiago Fernández de Valderrama Aparicio built career-ops during his own 2026 job search and released it under the MIT license. In July 2026 he published the [CareerOps Manifesto](https://career-ops.org/manifesto), which names the practice and nine candidate rights.

Repositories tagged `career-ops` include the core project and community plugins, translations, dashboards, and companion tools.
