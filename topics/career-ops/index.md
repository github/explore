career-ops is a free, open source job search system for candidates. It runs inside an AI coding assistant such as Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode, or in an optional local web interface. Paste a job posting and it checks whether the listing is still open, scores the role from 1.0 to 5.0 against your own CV, and suggests skipping roles below 4.0. It drafts a tailored CV and cover letter and tracks each application on your machine, with no account and no telemetry. It never submits or sends anything, so you review every draft and make every decision.

Santiago Fernández de Valderrama Aparicio built career-ops during his own 2026 job search and released it under the MIT license.

Repositories tagged `career-ops` include the core project and community plugins, translations, dashboards, and companion tools.
