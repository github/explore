Platform Engineering is the discipline of designing, building, and maintaining toolchains and workflows that enable self-service capabilities for software engineering organizations. By providing Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs), centralized developer portals, and infrastructure automation tooling, platform engineers drastically reduce cognitive load for developers, ensure standard compliance, and accelerate overall software delivery.
