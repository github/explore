Aspire is an open source, code-first toolchain for distributed apps. You describe projects, containers, services, and databases as code; Aspire runs them together locally (with a dashboard for logs, traces, and metrics) and provisions/deploys those same resources to your chosen target environment. Extensible and agent-ready.
