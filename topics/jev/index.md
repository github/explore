Jev is an AI decision model developed by TypeSafe AI. 
Unlike traditional large language models that generate text, Jev is designed to make typed decisions, classifications, and probability-based judgments. 
It can be integrated into applications, developer tools, and AI agents for tasks where a structured decision is more useful than generated prose.
